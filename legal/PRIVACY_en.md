# AG. AI TOKENS — Privacy Notice

**Version 1.0 — effective 12 November 2026** · under arts. 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"). The Italian text is the authoritative version.

## 1. Data controller
DevPeps, author of AG. AI TOKENS. Contact: the project page https://github.com/cPeps/AGTokens (Issues) or the Security section for confidential requests.

## 2. Basic principle: your projects stay on your PC
The Program processes your projects, your code, your conversations with AIs, the clipboard and window names **only on your PC**: this data is **never sent** to the controller or to third parties. The Program's proxy only works with AI models installed on your PC.

## 3. Data processed by the license server
To check the license the Program sends to the controller's server **only**:
- the **activation key**;
- an **anonymous PC fingerprint** (SHA-256 code computed from technical PC data; it contains no names, users, files or addresses);
- the Program **version** and the fingerprint of the Program file (to recognise modified copies);
- the **version of the Terms and Privacy Notice accepted** and the date of acceptance.
The server also records the date of the first and last check. It **does not record IP addresses**, names, emails or contents.
Although pseudonymous, this data may be considered personal data, so it is processed according to the GDPR.

If you request a key on GitHub, the nickname you write in the request is public on GitHub and is used only to send you the key.

## 4. Purposes and legal bases
- License check, "one key = one PC" rule, expiry and blocking of keys: **performance of the contract** (art. 6.1.b GDPR).
- Recognising modified copies and preventing abuse: the controller's **legitimate interest** in protecting the Program (art. 6.1.f).
- Distributing signed updates and messages to users: performance of the contract.
- Keeping proof of acceptance of the Terms: legitimate interest and legal obligations (art. 6.1.c / 6.1.f).
Reading technical PC information is strictly necessary for the service requested by the User (license check).

## 5. Providers and transfers
Requests pass through the infrastructure of **Cloudflare, Inc.** (relay and HTTPS tunnel), which processes technical network data (e.g. IP address) under its own privacy policy, also outside the EU with the safeguards provided (Standard Contractual Clauses / Data Privacy Framework). Updates and project pages are hosted on **GitHub, Inc.** under its own privacy policy. The controller does not sell or transfer data to third parties.

## 6. Retention
Key data is kept for the life of the key and deleted automatically when the PC has made no checks for **12 months**. Proof of acceptance of the Terms is kept for the same period, unless needed to defend legal claims.

## 7. Security
Communications are encrypted (HTTPS); server answers and updates are digitally signed; the server panel is only accessible from the controller's PC with a password.

## 8. Your rights
You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability (arts. 15-22 GDPR), stating your key (needed to find the data, which does not contain your name). Erasing the data disables the key. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (**Garante per la protezione dei dati personali**, www.garanteprivacy.it) or your local authority.

## 9. Minors
The Program is intended for adults or minors with the consent of a parent or guardian.

## 10. Changes
New versions of this notice are published on GitHub and shown in the Program.
